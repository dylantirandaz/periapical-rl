What counts as a pass?

Find

Every lesion. A miss and a false alarm both cost.

Box

One-to-one at IoU ≥ 0.3. Extra boxes count against; boxing everything scores ≈ 0.

Grade

PAI 3, 4 or 5, exact. ±1 tolerance would make PAI 4 always right.

Score

½ lesion F1 + ½ graded F1. Pass only at 1.
