Inkling is learning to find infections at the tips of tooth roots on dental X-rays and rate how severe they are, in two stages. First it is fine-tuned on 2,587 X-rays the dentists labelled; then reinforcement learning practises on 500 X-rays it has not seen, 16 at a time, 8 tries each. The benchmark score is 1.0 only when every box overlaps the dentists' box and every severity matches (half boxes, half severities). Progress is measured on 389 validation X-rays; the 387 test X-rays are scored once, at the end. Dentists checked against each other score 0.84.
